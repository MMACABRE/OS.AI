Phosphor motion

Live previews of the motion rules in spec/design/motion.md, in the Phosphor look. The stage below stacks the real mockups: the background is one layer that keeps running and never moves (MOT-7), the page contents slide and fade toward the side of the switcher they leave to (MOT-5), and the title and status bars stay put while the switcher's yellow marker slides to the new page (MOT-6). Use the buttons or Ctrl+1, Ctrl+2, Ctrl+3.

Page change: 420 ms, slide 96 px with a fade, ease out.
