Module: hud

One glance tells the owner which Claude sessions need them, what each is doing, how much of the usage limits is left, and which skill to reach for. It replaces the owner's agent-hud mod.

Session ribbon

Claude Code prints its model and mascot at the top of the chat, and they scroll away. The ribbon keeps them in view above every Claude terminal. See layout and terminal.

  • HUD-80 draws the ribbon, 28 px high.
  • HUD-82 lists running subagents with their mascot.
  • HUD-83 moves as motion says.
  • Voice alerts follow voice-setup.
